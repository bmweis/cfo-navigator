The Real Trade-off: Lookahead

Landscape shows more of the channel ahead—more reaction time to obstacles. Portrait is narrower, so there's inherently less warning. That's not a bug to fix, it's a real difference between orientations. Recommending obstacle/gust timing be generated in time-until-reach rather than fixed pixel distance, so a portrait player isn't unfairly punished by a compressed view—reaction window stays roughly consistent even though what's visible differs.
