Rowing burns energy—modeled as Runway drain—so a fast, mostly-rowed run scores lower than a slightly slower, mostly-sailed one. Exact weighting between time and energy is a Phase 0 decision, not guessed here.
Skyline is now SVG (window-grid texture on the tall tower, atmospheric fade on the far layer, a soft mirrored reflection on the water) instead of flat CSS rectangles—same fix category as the boat. Difficulty is rank-based: Deckhand → Mate → First Mate → Skipper. Score display replaces the old sail%-only readout with a single composite number, backed by time and energy-spent as the two visible inputs.
