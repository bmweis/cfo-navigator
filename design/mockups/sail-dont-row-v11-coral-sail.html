Coral Sail, No Flag

Sail now matches the former pennant color. Pennant removed—sail carries that color pop on its own. Fold-line shading recolored to work on coral instead of the old white sail (cream highlight, deep rust shadow, rather than the seafoam lines that were tuned for white cloth).

Kept the seafoam waterline stripe on the hull so the boat still carries some brand color beyond navy, now that the sail's moved off-palette into coral. If the rock/obstacle color collision bothers you once you see it in motion in the actual game, an easy fix is nudging the rock color slightly warmer/darker so the two read as clearly different at a glance—didn't want to make that call unprompted.
